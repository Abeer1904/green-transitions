# Solar pathway: field review

Use the local site with one learner and one facilitator before expanding this experience to other technologies. No real partner offer is sent by the prototype.

## Learner — 20 minutes
1. Start at Home. Find the solar irrigation map without coaching.
2. Explain the PV array, controller, pump and tank in your own words. Use the enlarged view only if needed.
3. Explore a cloudy condition, then pause the animation. What does the illustration tell you? What does it not establish?
4. Complete the four practice exercises. Record any confusing words or feedback.
5. Choose a role, enter an example work sample, and review the CV. Explain the difference between learning activity and assessed practical work.
6. Download or print the CV. Check legibility and whether anything appears to claim a qualification incorrectly.

## Facilitator — 20 minutes
1. Choose the community role in Framework. Open a stage and write its output.
2. Record a local problem, responsibility, evidence and next step.
3. Visit the course capstone and a village pathway. Add a decision and owner.
4. Create a pipeline draft. Verify that evidence and pathway notes transferred correctly.
5. Export the saved work. Explain where the data is stored and whether a partner received anything.

## Observe on phones
- Can a user find the two starting routes and the next action?
- Are component labels readable with enlargement and the text inspector?
- Can exercises and forms be used without sideways page scrolling?
- Are touch targets, text size and contrast comfortable outdoors?
- Does the language match local usage? New pages still need reviewed Hindi translation.
- Ask women learners which practical access barriers are missing; do not infer preferences from gender.

Record task success, hesitation, misunderstood terms and the user's own suggested changes. Do not treat the prototype's completion markers as field validation or practical competence.
