A plant does not run by itself. The directory describes six skills. Some are technical, some are about daily operation, some are business, and one is about keeping records.

## 1. Plant mason

**Builds the plant.** Local masons who already work with brick and cement can be re-skilled. Training comes through the **Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)**, a grassroots training centre that brings scientific know-how to farmers and rural youth. **KVK Faridabad** is the closest at district level. The **IIT Delhi Biogas Development and Training Centre** also trains across Haryana, UP, Uttarakhand and Delhi.

**Who can join:** age 18–45. Being able to read and write Hindi or English, or having passed 8th standard, is preferred. Basic masonry skill is needed.

## 2. Plant operator

**Runs the plant every day.** Feeding, watching for leaks, gas safety. Training comes from the IIT Delhi centre or the Hydrocarbon Sector Skill Council. It has two national qualifications for compressed biogas: *Plant Operator* and *Plant Technician*.

**Who can join:** age 18 or more, plus one of several routes, such as 11th pass, or 10th pass with one year of relevant experience, or 8th pass with three years of relevant experience.

## 3. Feedstock aggregator

**Collects the dung and crop residue** from dairy households and gaushalas. Judges quality and quantity, agrees a price, stores and sells. The **Skill Council for Green Jobs** teaches an *Agri-residue Aggregator* course: 10 hours, NSQF Level 3.

## 4. Bio-slurry processor and packager

**Turns the by-product into a product.** It is both a technical and a business skill. Slurry goes to farms, which connects to the regenerative agriculture track. **CCS Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar** runs 4-week hands-on courses on drying, separating and enriching slurry into fermented organic manure. Graduates can pitch for **grants of up to ₹25 lakh** for agri-startups. Short courses through KVK networks run from free to about ₹5,000.

## 5. Safety and compliance recorder

**Keeps the records that keep people safe.** Training logs, gas-check records before anyone enters a digester, readings from methane and hydrogen sulphide monitors, subsidy paperwork, panchayat records. Training routes are longer: **420–480 hours** at NSQF level, plus a mandatory **30-hour** health and safety module on the National Qualification Register.

## 6. Briquette or pellet maker (optional)

**Presses farm waste into solid fuel blocks.** A separate business that can sit beside a biogas plant. An online **Biomass Briquettes Business** course (9 lectures, MSME certificate) is offered by the Institute of Industrial Development. Panipat is a briquette hub.

:::warn
Check before you plan
The directory names two different sector councils for the safety-recorder training (one appears to be a healthcare council, the other hydrocarbons). Confirm the right body and course with the training provider before anyone enrols.
:::

:::ask
Talk about it together

- Who in your village already has a masonry, farming, record-keeping or selling skill that fits one of these six?
- Which of the six could a woman in an SHG do first?
:::
