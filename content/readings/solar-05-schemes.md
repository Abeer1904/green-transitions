## Who can use solar

- **Farmers**, for 3–10 HP pumps.
- **Households**, for rooftop systems under PM Surya Ghar.
- **Panchayats**, for institutions and streetlights.
- **Cooperatives**, for a community pump, or a small decentralised plant under KUSUM Component A.

What it can do: an irrigation system **without diesel** saves money. Where net metering or feed-in works, **households can earn from surplus electricity.**

## PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Subsidises **grid-connected rooftop solar on homes.**

| System size | Subsidy |
|---|---|
| First 2 kW | ₹30,000 per kW |
| 2–3 kW | ₹18,000 per kW |
| 3 kW or more | Capped at **₹78,000** in total |

There is no extra subsidy above 3 kW. *(Try the numbers in the "0→1" view.)*

## PM KUSUM: three components

**Component A: a small solar power plant on spare land.**
Farmers, panchayats, FPOs and cooperatives set up a 0.5–2 MW grid-connected plant on **barren or uncultivable land** and sell power to the DISCOM. For landowners or co-operatives with land within **5 km of a substation**. Financed by the farmer or developer. The DISCOM buys at a pre-fixed tariff.

**Component B: standalone solar pumps to replace diesel.**
Pumps up to 7.5–15 HP for areas **without grid supply**. For individual off-grid farmers. Centre and state each give **30%** subsidy; the farmer pays the rest (30–40%, partly loanable).

**Component C: solarise an existing grid pump.**
The farmer uses solar by day and grid at night, and can **sell surplus to the DISCOM** under net metering. Includes **Feeder-Level Solarisation (FLS)**, where the state solarises a whole agricultural feeder, which is simpler to do village-wide.

:::note
Malai's farmers use grid-fed tubewells
That is what the directory says. Think about which component fits a farmer who is *already on the grid*. Component B is written for areas *without* grid supply.
:::

:::warn
Amounts and rules change
Subsidy rates and eligibility are as the directory reports them. Confirm with HAREDA or the DISCOM before anyone commits money.
:::

:::ask
Talk about it together

- Which farmers have a grid-connected tubewell? Who has a diesel pump?
- Is there any barren land near a substation that the panchayat could use?
:::
