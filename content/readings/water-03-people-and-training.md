Rain capture is built by masons, checked by testers, supervised by a technical lead and kept going by people who watch the water. The directory names five roles.

## The roles

- **Rooftop rainwater harvesting mason or plumber.** Designs, builds and maintains storage tanks, filter chambers and recharge pits. Needs to understand roof slope, runoff, and how to fit secure PVC downpipes and gutters. The work gives masons and construction workers income in dry and drought seasons.
- **Water quality tester.** Collects water samples and tests them for chemical, physical and biological contaminants. It protects people from drinking or farming with contaminated water, in homes and on farms.
- **Pond or johad survey and rejuvenation supervisor.** Needs technical knowledge of civil works. Oversees the restoration of a damaged water body from start to finish.
- **Recharge structure builder.** Builds the underground structure that filters roof or surface runoff and sends it into the soil to refill the aquifer. Needs basic masonry. MGNREGS workers build these.
- **Watershed monitor.** Collects, analyses and tracks water, weather and ecological data to judge the health of the catchment. The directory gives no training route for this role yet.

## Where people train

- **Mason or plumber.** **Rural Mason Training** (Ministry of Rural Development) skills local masons in durable building that includes decentralised water structures. The **Haryana Skill Development Mission** offers multi-skill construction training through **Swaranjayanti Kaushal Vikas Kendras (SJKVK)**.
- **Water quality tester.** **NIFTEM, Sonipat** houses the Centre for Food Research and Analysis, with workshops on physico-chemical parameters and trace contamination in water and food. **PHED Haryana** trains rural communities to use **field testing kits**. The **Agriculture Skill Council of India** runs an NSQF-aligned programme for **Soil and Water Testing Lab Technician**: processing samples, preparing solutions, data entry and using testing kits. Courses cost roughly **₹3,000 to ₹7,000**.
- **Johad supervisor.** The **Haryana Pond and Waste Water Management Authority (HPWWMA)** is the state body for supervision, quality inspection and survey coordination. It releases tenders, so people trained in rejuvenation supervision can work with it.

:::note
Two gaps
The recharge builder and watershed monitor roles have no named course. The directory lists them as skills but gives no training route, cost or certificate.
:::
