Natural farming spreads through people: a trainer farmers trust, someone who makes the inputs, someone who tests the soil. The directory names six roles.

## The roles

- **Natural farming trainer.** A trained community resource person who coaches small and marginal farmers from chemical-heavy farming to low-cost methods, peer to peer. They link research institutions, government schemes and the village.
- **Compost or vermicompost unit operator.** Runs the daily work of turning organic waste into fertiliser with earthworms and natural decomposition: preparing feedstock, managing beds, caring for stock, harvesting, processing and maintenance.
- **Bio-input maker.** Makes natural inputs such as jeevamrit, beejamrit, neem preparations and panchgavya.
- **Soil health tester and advisor.** Analyses soil and advises on fertiliser use, crop choice and land management.
- **Water irrigation advisor.** Uses weather data, soil sensors and crop models to set watering schedules and cut waste.
- **Agroforestry or nursery worker.** The directory names the role and gives no description.

## Where people train

- **Trainer.** The National Mission on Natural Farming and the Krishi Sakhi initiative of the Ministry of Rural Development train community resource persons to become certified master trainers.
- **Compost operator.** Krishi Vigyan Kendras run hands-on courses on setting up and managing a vermicompost unit. The Institute of Industrial Development offers a vermicompost course with MSME government certification.
- **Bio-input maker.** The National Institute of Plant Health Management trains people to set up and run bio-input resource centres.
- **Soil tester.** HAMETI and CCS Haryana Agricultural University run 15-day certified courses for ground-level workers.
- **Irrigation advisor.** The Water Technology Centre at ICAR-IARI, Pusa, New Delhi, runs a 3 to 6 month course on micro-irrigation and climate-smart water management.

:::note
Gaps
The directory gives no cost for any of these courses and nothing for the agroforestry role. Ask each institute for current fees and dates.
:::
