Panels on a roof are only the start. Someone has to install them, clean them, find faults, run a shared pump and check that the system suits the need.

## 1. Solar PV installer / technician

Sets up, tests, maintains and repairs solar systems. The idea is to **re-skill village technicians and train young people who want work without leaving the village.**

**Where to train**
- **Suryamitra Skill Development Programme.** A national flagship to train rural youth. 3 months (600 hours): classroom, lab practicals, entrepreneurship skills and plant visits.
- **Solis Energy Solutions, Faridabad.** A 40-day package for **₹9,200**.
- **Surya Urza Enterprise, Hisar.**

**Who can join Suryamitra:** age 18 or above, with ITI (Electrician, Wireman, Electronics Mechanic) or a diploma in electrical, mechanical or electronics.

:::warn
A real barrier
Several Suryamitras have said the programme is **long and residential**. Many are the sole earner, so it is hard to leave work and family for months. A study (Tyagi et al., 2022) also called for more hands-on, on-the-job training. When you plan training for your village, ask whether people can actually attend.
:::

## 2. Solar pump installer and O&M technician

Does site layout, mounts the panels, sets up the pump structure, and connects controllers, inverters and wiring. Afterwards: routine checks, cleaning panels, watching the controller and fixing faults. Knowing the **PM KUSUM** scheme is a big plus.

**Training:** the **Varunmitra** programme at the **National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE), Gurugram**. 3 weeks (21 days, 120 hours). It gives **special preference to rural candidates, unemployed youth, women, and SC/ST communities.** Usual background: ITI or an electrical or mechanical diploma.

## 3. Panel cleaning and basic fault-finding

Washing panels the right way, with the right tools, so they do not get micro-cracks. Spotting a fault: an inverter problem, a junction box problem, a drop in output. **NISE trains SHG women and youth** who can offer this as a paid service. Suryamitra also runs short specialised courses.

## 4. Solar pump operator and tariff collector

Only for a **community-owned pump**. Someone keeps it working and collects the water fee. Trained through Varunmitra (3-week residential, government sponsored). The **National Power Training Institute, Faridabad**, also runs short renewable energy courses.

## 5. Energy auditor

Inspects, measures and analyses pump size, irrigation timing and the load. Certification is managed by the **Bureau of Energy Efficiency**, under the Ministry of Power.
