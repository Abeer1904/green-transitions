## What is solar PV?

**Solar photovoltaic (PV)** panels turn sunlight into electricity. That electricity can run:

- lights and appliances in a **home**,
- the **panchayat building and streetlights**,
- a **water pump** for irrigation.

## Why it matters in Malai

The directory describes Malai's electricity as **unreliable**:

- Farmers depend on **grid-fed tubewells** for irrigation.
- Household supply is only about **12 hours a day**.
- Some irrigation still runs on **diesel**, which costs money every season.

Solar speaks directly to the **reliability gap**, and it cuts the diesel cost for irrigation.

## Start with the problem, not the panel

Notice that "solar" answers three different problems: a household that wants light in the evening, a farmer who wants water at the right time, and a panchayat that wants its school and streetlights to work. **Each one needs a different system, different people, and a different owner.**
