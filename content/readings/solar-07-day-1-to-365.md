Use the **0→1 view** to walk through this with pictures. Here it is in words.

**Day 1–30: Test and map.**
Test **water salinity**. Test **solar irradiance** (how much sun the site gets). Map the village's solar needs.

**Day 31–75: Find the help and the site.**
Identify a **scheme facilitator**. Meet the farmer group and HAREDA/UHBVN. Shortlist sites.

**Day 76–180: Train and demonstrate.**
Train youth through **vendor courses**. Do a **demonstration pump installation** at a household.

**Day 181–270: Collect and maintain.**
Start **tariff collection** for the community pump.

**Day 271–330: Second batch.**
A **second batch of village trainees**, followed by **more household applications.**

**Day 331–365: Assess.**
Assess the year's work, and how ready the village is to become a **PM Surya Ghar Model Solar Village.**

:::note
One path, not the only one
The important habit is the order: *test, then plan, then train, then run, then review.* If the water test says no to a pump, a good plan changes. That is not failure. It is the plan working.
:::
