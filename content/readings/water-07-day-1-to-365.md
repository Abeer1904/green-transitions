The directory gives a short plan for the first year. Use it as a starting order, not a fixed calendar.

**Months 1 to 2: map and assess.**
Map the existing pond sites and the household water sources. Bring in a technical partner to assess them.

**Months 3 to 6: protect and restore.**
Secure a community **catchment protection agreement**. Restore the catchment with an **MGNREGA** crew.

**Months 7 to 12: watch and fix.**
Monitor the water captured and fix problems. Track the **groundwater level** to see whether it changes.

:::note
The year ends where the work starts
Groundwater changes slowly. One monsoon will not show a clear result, so keep the record book going into year two.
:::

:::ask
Talk about it together

- Who would sign a catchment protection agreement in your village?
- What would you measure in month 7 to know the work is helping?
:::
