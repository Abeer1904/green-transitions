These are the examples the directory gives. They show that the work can be done. They give no costs, incomes or results over time.

## Ponds that reuse household water

- **Barokhan village, Haryana.** A pond system recycles household wastewater for irrigation and has cut dependence on groundwater.
- **Bhainsru Kalan, Rohtak.** Wastewater from households is channelled into a five-pond management system.

## Johads brought back

- **Dhikoli village, Uttar Pradesh.** A restored johad was repaired with a reed bed and returned to everyday use. It feeds two new ponds, and the community supplied the labour.
- **Rithal johad, Rohtak.** A well-documented community johad that still works in the region.

:::note
What is missing
None of these examples says who pays for upkeep, whether anyone earns from it, or how the water level changed. The directory also gives no source for them. Confirm each place before you quote it.
:::
