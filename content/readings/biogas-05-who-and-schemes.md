## Who can do it

- **Households with 3 or more cattle.** As a rule of thumb, a **2 m³ plant** is fed by about **50 kg of dung a day**, which is roughly **4–5 animals**.
- **SHG women's groups, dairy cooperatives and Farmer Producer Organisations.** Groups can pool dung and share a plant.
- **Youth** as operators and technicians.

## What can be done

- Replace LPG and firewood for cooking. Better health, less spending, less smoke.
- Make **organic manure** as a by-product, and turn it into a small business.
- Start with a household model, then join up into a community or CBG plant *if the feedstock justifies it.*

## Two schemes to know

- **GOBARdhan** (Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan). Part of the Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen), run with several ministries together. Aim: convert cattle dung, crop residue and other organic waste into biogas, compressed biogas and manure, so that village waste becomes an asset that can be sold. There is a single national portal, **gobardhan.co.in**, to register plants. Incentives are based on the number of households in the Gram Panchayat.
- **National Bioenergy Programme** (MNRE). The main funding line for small and medium biogas plants and off-grid bio-power and waste-to-energy projects.
