The directory sets out one possible year. These stages adapt the directory’s example sequence. They are readiness steps, not a fixed one-year course.

Use the **0→1 view** to walk through it with pictures. Here it is in words.

**Stage: Survey.**
Survey cattle, dung and crop residue. Also survey **women's cooking fuel use**. Together these make a *feedstock map* and show which households could benefit.

**Stage: Organise and register.**
Decide: household plants, or a community plant? For a community plant, form an SHG or a cooperative. Register with GOBARdhan or HAREDA. Shortlist constructors.

**Stage: Build and train.**
Install **5–10 household plants or 1 community plant**. Train **7–8 people** on site as operators.

**Stage: Run and earn.**
Set a fee roster for the workers. Pilot **slurry packaging** with farmers as buyers. This gives the first income.

**Stage: Grow.**
Train a **second batch** of village youth. Test a **briquette add-on**. More users, more farmers, more income.

**Stage: Review.**
Keep records. Review **income and spending**. Track how many people are trained.

:::note
This is one path, not the only one
Every village is different. Steps can overlap, or need repeating. What matters is the *order of thinking*: know your feedstock, organise the people, build, train, earn, then check whether it is holding up.
:::
