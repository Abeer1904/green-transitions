## The steps, in order

1. **Map the load.** What needs power: homes, farms, or public buildings like the school and streetlights?
2. **Choose the site.** Panchayat land, or private property? Who controls access?
3. **For a solar pump: check the water first.** Find the **groundwater category** and test **water salinity** *before* sizing the pump. Under PM KUSUM, in **over-exploited zones only the conversion of existing pumps with micro-irrigation is allowed.**
4. **Apply** through the portal or HAREDA.
5. **Vendors install and train** the youth who will look after maintenance.
6. **Build a technician pool** and set up coordination between everyone involved.

## What it costs

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Site survey (roof or land, shading, borewell depth) and application | ₹5,000 to about ₹10,000 |
| PM KUSUM / PM Surya Ghar registration | Free, through the portal |
| Household's own share after subsidy, 1–3 kW | ₹25,000–₹87,000 |
| PM KUSUM pump (3–10 HP), farmer's share | ₹54,000–₹1.1 lakh |
| Technicians, while in training | ₹15,000–₹30,000 |
| Technicians, on a formal salary | ₹10,000–₹15,000 |

:::note
Read these as a guide
The site-survey figure is cut off in the directory ("₹5,000–₹10,00"). Treat ₹10,000 as an approximation and confirm. Ask for a written quote from any vendor.
:::

:::ask
Talk about it together

- Which of your fields or roofs could be a site? Who owns it?
- Has anyone tested their tubewell water? What do you know about the water level?
:::
